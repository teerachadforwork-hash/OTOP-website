## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a contemporary celebration of Thai heritage craftsmanship, rooted in authentic community culture while functioning as a clean, highly credible modern digital commerce platform. It fuses tactile craft textures with editorial precision and seamless e-commerce interaction patterns.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Local and international patrons seeking authentic Thai geographical indication (GI) products, handcrafted textiles, natural apothecary, and gourmet local delicacies; alongside generational artisan makers needing an accessible, empowering showcase.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires deep cultural respect, discovery, warmth, and reliable commercial trust. Every screen must feel like entering a curated boutique gallery infused with sun-baked pottery, natural plant dyes, and woven bamboo architecture.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Tactile Modernist Craft & Editorial Commerce:** A hybrid of organic tactile warmth and structural Scandinavian-Japanese e-commerce rigor. Generous warm-cream whitespace, structured framing lines reminiscent of traditional loom warps, bespoke artisan credentials, and tactile micro-interactions that elevate local crafts to global premium retail standards.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural raw materials utilized across Thailand’s four artisan regions: baked clay, mor hom indigo dye, dried golden vetiver straw, rich tropical soils, and rainforest vegetation.

### Core Palette Application
- **Primary (`#B85434` - Terracotta Clay):** Represents natural earthenware and kiln craftsmanship. Used for primary calls-to-action, active price indicators, cart triggers, and highlighted interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#2B4764` - Phrae Indigo Weave):** Represents natural plant-dyed cotton and northern Thai textile heritage. Used for secondary interaction controls, header toolbars, provenance titles, artisan profiles, and technical specifications.
- **Tertiary (`#D19C4C` - Golden Straw & Harvest):** Represents sun-dried wickerwork, bamboo weave, and the Thai 5-Star OTOP quality designation. Used for star ratings, official product grade stamps, seasonal promotions, and subtle badge borders.
- **Neutral Core (`#24272B` - Charcoal Slate):** Provides high-contrast, crisp legibility across typography, iconography, and structured container rules, avoiding harsh pure black.

### Supporting Semantic Tokens
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Rice Paper):** Primary application backdrop; delivers an organic, glare-free reading surface.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Linen):** Used for elevated cards, active modals, and checkout drawer sheets.
- **Forest Provenance (`#2F6B4F` - Eco & Community Green):** Explicitly reserved for community-certified badges, Organic Thailand seals, Geographic Indication (GI) verification tags, and sustainable material certifications.
- **Subtle Stroke & Border (`#E6E0D5` - Loom Thread Neutral):** Delivers clean structural division without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Epilogue** for display, category headers, and editorial titles, pairing its grounded geometric structure with warm, expressive craft heritage. For all body narrative copy, e-commerce pricing, specifications, and UI badges, **Be Vietnam Pro** delivers optimal cross-platform legibility, high humanist open counters, and seamless bilingual Thai-Latin reading rhythm.

### Editorial & Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Artisan Narrative Titles:** Headlines are set in terracotta (`#B85434`) or indigo (`#2B4764`) to give weight to community stories and master craftsman lineages.
- **Product Title Pairings:** Product cards employ `headline-sm` with strict truncation to two lines, backed by `caption` provenance tags tracking the craft's tambon, district, and province.
- **Numerics & Currencies:** All currency figures (฿ THB) utilize `Be Vietnam Pro` in semi-bold weight to ensure numeric clarity across all inventory and checkout calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adapted 12-column grid system bounded inside an editorial maximum width of `1280px`. The system provides spacious margins that allow artisanal product photography to breathe while maintaining high conversion velocity.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grid Rhythm
- **Mobile (Viewport < 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutters `16px`, margins `16px`. Dense 2-column grid for product feeds; single-column full-bleed layout for storytelling artisan features.
- **Tablet (Viewport 768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Gutters `24px`, margins `32px`. Product listings reflow to 3-column layouts; split artisan showcase cards (image 4 cols / story copy 4 cols).
- **Desktop (Viewport > 1024px):** 12-column layout. Gutters `32px`, margins `48px`. Product catalog spans 4 columns; detail view provides a 7-column gallery and a 5-column sticky purchase/provenance module.

### Spacing Cadence
Layouts prioritize vertical breathing room between editorial story sections (`space-3xl` / 72px) and concise grouping within functional transaction blocks (`space-sm` to `space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on sterile grey drop-shadows. Instead, depth is established through tonal layering, surface color warmth, and ambient shadows tinted with raw umber and warm clay.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAF7F2` (Warm Rice Paper). Un-elevated base container.
2. **Level 1 (Content Surfaces & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Linen) bounded by a delicate 1px border of `#E6E0D5`. Zero blur shadow when idle, preserving flat craft clarity.
3. **Level 2 (Hover State & Interactive Floating Elements):**
   - Box shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(184, 84, 52, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 39, 43, 0.04)`.
   - Border color transitions to `#D19C4C` (Golden Straw) at 50% opacity.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Checkout Sheets & Overlays):**
   - Box shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(43, 71, 100, 0.16), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 39, 43, 0.06)`.
   - Backdrop filter: `blur(8px)` with 40% opacity overlay of `#24272B`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a roundedness level of `2` (Moderate Rounded), balancing contemporary digital cleanliness with the organic, rounded silhouettes of handmade ceramics, woven baskets, and hand-carved teak spoons.

### Geometry Parameters
- **Base Components (Buttons, Input Fields, Badges):** `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Cards & Story Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `16px` (`1rem`) corner radius.
- **Hero Containers & Slide Sheets (`rounded-xl`):** `24px` (`1.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Pills / Status Badges:** Fully circular (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for star ratings, geographic origin indicators, and artisan avatar portraits.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Purchase / Add to Cart):** Background `#B85434` (Terracotta), text `#FFFFFF`, height `48px`, font weight 600. On hover, background deepens to `#A04529` with subtle clay elevation.
- **Secondary Action (Inquire Maker / Discover Story):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #2B4764` (Indigo), text `#2B4764`. Hover states shift background to `rgba(43, 71, 100, 0.05)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure text `#B85434` with an underline transition and right arrow chevron, used for inline editorial discovery.

### Artisan & Quality Badges
- **5-Star OTOP Standard Badge:** Pill container, background `rgba(209, 156, 76, 0.12)`, text `#9C6E23`, border `1px solid rgba(209, 156, 76, 0.35)`. Contains gold star icon followed by official OTOP star rating designation.
- **Geographic Indication & Eco Tag:** Background `rgba(47, 107, 79, 0.1)`, text `#2F6B4F`, border `1px solid rgba(47, 107, 79, 0.25)`. Features a leaf or GIS marker icon.
- **Province Pill:** Background `#F2ECE2`, text `#2B4764`, font size 12px, font weight 600. Shows the provenance (e.g., "เชียงใหม่ / Chiang Mai").

### Storytelling Product Cards
- Elevated on white background with 16px corner radius and `1px solid #E6E0D5` stroke.
- **Image Module:** Aspect ratio 4:3 or 1:1, warm neutral fallback background, featuring a floating province tag at the top left and a heart bookmark button at the top right.
- **Content Area:** 
  - Overline artisan group name (e.g., "กลุ่มทอผ้าบ้านดอนกอย") in `body-sm` muted charcoal.
  - Product title in `headline-sm`.
  - Star rating component with active count.
  - Price display in bold `#B85434` paired with an immediate "Add" action button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E6E0D5`, placeholder in muted neutral (`#888B8E`). On active focus: border transitions to `#2B4764` with an indigo ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(43, 71, 100, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded square (4px) or full circle; checked fill `#B85434` featuring crisp white SVG checks.

### Origin Filter Chips
- Flexible horizontal scrolling filter bar. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E6E0D5` outline. Selected: filled Indigo `#2B4764` with white text and quick dismiss icon.
